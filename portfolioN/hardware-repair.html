Hardware Repair & Troubleshooting

Updated: August 2025 • 12 min read

Overview: This guide covers common hardware issues and practical fixes for desktops and laptops. Based on 5+ years of hands-on computer repair experience.

1 Laptop Not Powering On

FIX

Check Power Connection

Ensure the power adapter is securely connected to both the laptop and the wall outlet.

FIX

Perform a Hard Reset

Remove the battery (if removable), unplug the power cord, hold the power button for 30 seconds, then reconnect.

FIX

Test with Another Charger

If possible, try a different charger to rule out a faulty power adapter.

2 PC Overheating

FIX

Clean the Fans

Use compressed air to remove dust from CPU and case fans. Do this in a well-ventilated area.

FIX

Check Thermal Paste

Replace the thermal paste between the CPU and heatsink if it's old or dried out.

FIX

Improve Airflow

Ensure the PC has proper ventilation. Avoid placing it on carpet or in enclosed spaces.

3 Slow Hard Drive

FIX

Defragment the Drive

Run disk defragmentation (for HDDs only). In PowerShell: defrag C: /O

FIX

Free Up Space

Delete unnecessary files or move data to an external drive. Aim to keep at least 15% free space.

FIX

Consider SSD Upgrade

If performance is still poor, upgrading to an SSD is the most effective solution.

Diagnostic Tool

Use this PowerShell command to check your system's health:

# Get system health report

Get-WmiObject -Class Win32_ComputerSystem

This returns CPU, RAM, and system status information.

Safety Note: Always unplug your device and discharge static electricity before opening the case. If you're unsure, consult a professional.
